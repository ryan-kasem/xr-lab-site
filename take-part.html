Take part in a study.

The XR Lab runs studies on how designed physical and virtual environments affect how people think, feel and behave. Studies take place in our lab at UC San Diego.

Where
SSRB 100, UC San Diego
What to expect
When you sign up, we explain what the study involves, how long it takes and who can take part.
Your choice
Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time.
Consent
Nothing starts until you have read and signed the consent form.
