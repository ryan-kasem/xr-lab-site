Our research.

The lab's work falls under two broad headings.

Biophilic Design in Architecture

How natural elements in built environments, such as plants, daylight, views and natural materials, relate to attention, stress and wellbeing, and how findings from cognitive neuroscience can inform architectural design.

  • Biophilia & Neurodiversity
  • Cognitive Neuroscience for Architecture (CNfA)
  • CNfA Chatbot

Cognition in Physical & Virtual Spaces

How people perceive, think and behave in physical rooms and in immersive virtual environments, and how spaces built in extended reality compare with the real places they model.

  • Testing Digital Twins
  • XR Lab Dev
  • Cognitive Tests in PsychoPy
  • Data Analytics
